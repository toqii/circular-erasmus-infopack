♻️ About the Project

CIRCULAR

Circular Transformation for Youth: Sustainability and New Horizons in the Digital Age

CIRCULAR is an Erasmus+ KA153-YOU Training Course that brings together youth workers from Türkiye, France, Spain and Slovenia to explore the intersection of circular economy, sustainability and digital transformation in youth work.

Through hands-on workshops, non-formal learning methods and international peer exchange, participants will develop practical competences in eco-design, repair culture, waste mapping and circular systems thinking — turning EU frameworks into actionable youth work activities.

The training course will take place in Antalya, Türkiye from 29 October to 3 November 2026, with arrival on 28 October and departure on 4 November.

🎯 Goals

Project Objectives

1

Strengthen youth workers' understanding of circular economy and sustainability.

2

Develop practical skills through eco-design, repair culture, waste mapping and circular systems thinking.

3

Turn EU concepts such as Right to Repair, Ecodesign and Greenwashing into practical youth work activities.

4

Co-create a Circular Youth Work Toolbox with ready-to-use methods and activities.

5

Improve youth workers' digital content creation and online facilitation skills.

6

Promote environmental awareness, inclusion and behavioural change among young people.

7

Strengthen peer learning and international cooperation between youth organisations.

8

Contribute to SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production and SDG 15 – Life on Land.

🙋 Eligibility

Who Can Participate?

✓

Be 18–50 years old. Group leaders have no age limit.

✓

Be active as a youth worker, volunteer, peer educator, trainer or educator working with young people.

✓

Have a clear interest in sustainability, circular economy and environmental issues.

✓

Be motivated to take part in non-formal learning activities, workshops and teamwork.

✓

Have at least an intermediate level of English to actively participate in the programme.

✓

Be willing to share the knowledge and methods gained during the project within their organisation or local community.

✓

Be interested in improving their digital skills and youth work practices.

✓

Be available for the full duration of the mobility and actively participate in all activities.

⭐ Priority Will Be Given To

Youth workers from rural communities
Youth workers from coastal communities
People from underrepresented communities
First-time Erasmus+ participants
People working with NEETs
People working with young people with fewer opportunities

🌊 Destination

Antalya, Türkiye

Antalya is a vibrant Mediterranean city on the southwest coast of Türkiye, known for its stunning turquoise coastline, rich cultural heritage and warm hospitality. As one of the most popular destinations on the Turkish Riviera, it offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and urban life.

With its mild autumn climate, ancient landmarks, and Mediterranean atmosphere, Antalya provides an inspiring setting for international youth work and intercultural exchange.
