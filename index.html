♻️ About the Project

CIRCULAR

Circular Transformation for Youth: Sustainability and New Horizons in the Digital Age

CIRCULAR is an Erasmus+ KA153-YOU Training Course that brings together youth workers from Türkiye, France, Spain and Slovenia to explore the intersection of circular economy, sustainability and digital transformation in youth work.

Through hands-on workshops, non-formal learning methods and international peer exchange, participants will develop practical competences in eco-design, repair culture, waste mapping and circular systems thinking — turning EU frameworks into actionable youth work activities.

The training course will take place in Antalya, Türkiye from 29 October to 3 November 2026, with arrival on 28 October and departure on 4 November.

🎯 Goals

Project Objectives

1

Strengthen youth workers' understanding of circular economy and sustainability.

2

Develop practical skills through eco-design, repair culture, waste mapping and circular systems thinking.

3

Turn EU concepts such as Right to Repair, Ecodesign and Greenwashing into practical youth work activities.

4

Co-create a Circular Youth Work Toolbox with ready-to-use methods and activities.

5

Improve youth workers' digital content creation and online facilitation skills.

6

Promote environmental awareness, inclusion and behavioural change among young people.

7

Strengthen peer learning and international cooperation between youth organisations.

8

Contribute to SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption and Production and SDG 15 – Life on Land.

🔑 Accommodation Rules

Room Sharing Policy

For safety, accommodation arrangements and programme rules, rooms may only be shared by participants of the same gender.

Participants of different genders are not permitted to share the same room, regardless of relationship status.

Participants are expected to stay in their assigned rooms and must not change rooms without informing and receiving approval from the organising team.

If an unauthorised room change occurs, or if a participant is required to change rooms without the knowledge of the organising team, the organisation may take the necessary measures in accordance with the project and accommodation rules, including applicable penalties.

🌊 Destination

Explore Antalya

Nature, history, Mediterranean culture and local cuisine

Antalya is a vibrant Mediterranean city on the southwest coast of Türkiye, known for its stunning turquoise coastline, rich cultural heritage and warm hospitality. As one of the most popular destinations on the Turkish Riviera, it offers a perfect blend of natural beauty and urban life.

With its mild autumn climate, ancient landmarks, and Mediterranean atmosphere, Antalya provides an inspiring setting for international youth work and intercultural exchange.

📍 Places to Discover

Must-See Spots

🌊

Düden Park & Düden Waterfalls

One of Antalya's most famous natural landmarks, where the Lower Düden Waterfall flows directly into the Mediterranean Sea.

🏛️

Kaleiçi (Old Town)

Antalya's historic centre with traditional houses, narrow streets, cafés, shops and the old harbour.

🏖️

Konyaaltı Beach

A long Mediterranean coastline with beautiful mountain views, perfect for walking and enjoying the sunset.

🍽️ Local Cuisine

Taste Antalya

🥙

Özkan Kebap

A good place to discover a wide variety of traditional Turkish dishes and kebabs.

🍦

Giliğ Dondurma / Akdeniz Dondurma

Try Antalya's traditional Yanık Dondurma (Burnt Ice Cream), a local ice cream made with goat's milk and known for its distinctive slightly smoky flavour.

⚠️ Important Notice About Alcohol

As most project activities will take place on the university campus, alcohol consumption is strictly prohibited inside the university and during project activities.

Participants must not bring, consume or distribute alcoholic beverages during sessions, National Evenings or any activity organised on university premises.

For National Evenings, you are welcome to bring traditional snacks, sweets, soft drinks, souvenirs and other cultural products from your country, but please do not bring alcohol.

We kindly ask all participants to respect the university rules and the professional learning environment of the project.
